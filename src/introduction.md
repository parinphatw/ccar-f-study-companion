## How to use this book

This book helps you reason about Claude applications: who controls execution, how tools expose capabilities, where instructions belong, how outputs are checked, and how long-running work remains reliable. The goal is to explain a design choice and its trade-offs, not merely recognize a product feature.

Read one short chapter, close it, and answer its recall questions aloud. Then solve the scenario before reading the answer section. Keep a mistake log with three columns: the requirement you missed, the tempting answer you chose, and the evidence that should have changed your decision. Revisit that log rather than repeatedly rereading familiar material.

The running example is Northstar Support, a fictional company building an assistant that researches customer orders, drafts support replies, and prepares narrowly authorized actions. A second example, a repository maintenance assistant, connects these principles to Claude Code. All scenarios and practice questions in this book are original teaching material, not recalled exam items.

1. Learn execution and delegation in Chapters 1 through 3.
2. Learn tool contracts and integrations in Chapters 4 and 5.
3. Learn project configuration and coding workflows in Chapters 6 and 7.
4. Learn prompting, output validation, and evaluation in Chapters 8 through 10.
5. Learn context and operational reliability in Chapters 11 and 12.
6. Complete the case exercises, explain every rejected option, and revisit the coverage checklist.
